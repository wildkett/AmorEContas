# App Store Page — Amor&Contas (English)

## App Name
Amor&Contas

## Subtitle
Split couple expenses fairly

## Short Description
Amor&Contas helps couples split expenses in a simple, transparent, and balanced way, with 50/50 or proportional scale-based split modes.

## Full Description
Amor&Contas was built to make everyday money conversations easier for couples.

With a clean and simple interface, you can add expenses, track how much each person paid, and instantly get a suggested settlement amount.

## Keywords
couple, expenses, split, bills, settle, shared expenses, budget, money

You can choose between two split modes:

- **50/50**: perfect when both people want an equal split, regardless of income.
- **Automatic (Scale)**: ideal for a proportional split. If you use each person’s net income as the scale value, the payment burden becomes similar for both — almost like paying with equal work time instead of equal money.

### Key Features
- Fast expense entry by person.
- Automatic fair-share calculation.
- Ready-to-share settlement message.
- Currency symbol settings.
- Split mode settings (50/50 or proportional).
- In-app “About” slides with motivation and usage guidance.

### Who it is for
- Couples splitting household bills, groceries, transportation, or leisure costs.
- People who want more clarity and less friction when managing shared expenses.

### Benefits
- Better financial transparency between partners.
- Fewer arguments about who paid more.
- Quick decision on how much one person should transfer.
